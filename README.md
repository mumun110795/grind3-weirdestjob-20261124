## About this entry

The title is **Head of Apologies for the Hallucinating Chatbot**. The angle is
deadpan corporate plausibility: "Head of …" is exactly how a real org chart
would phrase a senior role, yet the department it heads exists for one
narrow, very modern reason — a chatbot that invents facts with total
confidence and somebody senior who has to keep saying sorry for it. You can
picture the person instantly: calendar full of apology calls, a template for
every flavour of made-up policy, and a direct line to legal. It reads as one
HR department away from real, which is where the joke lands.
